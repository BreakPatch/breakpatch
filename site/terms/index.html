The licence check and usage counts

Breakpatch Solo and Team check the licence with our licence service when they open and about once a day. Usage counts are part of that check: every check from the app and from breakpatch-ci sends, per seat or machine, how many tests were created and runs made (by hand, from schedules, on the local runner and in CI, passed and failed) and on how many days Breakpatch was used. Your admins and Breakpatch see them in the back office. Nothing from inside a test is sent: no test names, addresses, steps or screenshots. There is no switch for them in Solo or Team.
