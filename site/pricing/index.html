Questions

What's a machine licence?
People take seats. A Mac that runs tests for the whole team (the local runner) or a build server that runs your tests takes a machine licence. Each machine licence runs one test at a time, so running two at once needs two. Team includes one. Extra ones are $40 a month, or $384 a year, always at list price.
For developers: A machine licence covers one test run at a time on any machine; a pipeline running N jobs in parallel needs N licences. A CI machine takes one when it runs breakpatch-ci.
Who is Solo for?
One person who wants the automation for themselves: fixed automatically, schedules on your Mac, notifications, result messages and the CI command line. It works on your tests folder or Git, with nothing to set up in the cloud. You can connect your own workspace if you want run requests, the local runner and version history, still for just you. Solo is one per company: if someone at your company email domain already has it, choose Team. Moving to Team later keeps your tests.
How do I get my licence key?
After checkout, sign in at account.breakpatch.dev with the email you bought with. Your key is shown there once. On Solo, enter it in Breakpatch, in Settings → Licence. On Team, a workspace admin enters it once in Settings → Licence. Then invite your team: the invite link sets up each person's licence, so they don't enter a key. The local runner and CI machines use a machine licence.
Can I change the number of people later?
Yes, from your account page. Adding or removing seats or machine licences is charged or credited straight away, pro rata for the rest of the billing period; credits go against your next payments. Cancel any time from Manage billing: the licence keeps working until the end of the period you paid for.
Who handles the payment?
Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all our orders, and handles payments, invoices, tax and refunds. For help with Breakpatch itself, email support@breakpatch.dev. You can ask either of us for a refund: see our refund policy.
