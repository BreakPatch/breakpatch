Refund policy

Last updated 30 September 2026.

Breakpatch Team and Business, and the Solo plan when it’s offered, are sold by our reseller Paddle.com, the Merchant of Record for all our orders, which handles refunds. This policy is our promise on top of your rights under the law where you live.

First payment: 14 days, no questions

If Breakpatch isn't right for you, ask for a refund within 14 days of your first payment and you get all of it back.

Renewals aren't refunded

Your subscription renews automatically at the end of each month or year. You can cancel at any time before the renewal date and you won't be charged again. Renewal payments aren't refunded: if you cancel after a renewal, the licence keeps working until the end of that period and then ends.

A second Solo at the same company

The Solo plan, when offered, is one per company. If you buy Solo and someone at your company email domain already has it, we refund your purchase in full. To work together, Team is the plan for more than one person.

Changes during a period

Adding or removing seats or machine licences is charged or credited straight away, pro rata for the rest of the billing period; credits go against your next payments. Cancelling stops the next renewal: the licence keeps working until the end of the period you paid for, and isn't refunded.

How to ask

  • Email support@breakpatch.dev from the address you bought with, or
  • find your order at paddle.net and ask Paddle directly.

Refunds go back to the card or account you paid with, usually within 5 to 10 business days. When a whole purchase is refunded, its licence ends and the people on it go back to Breakpatch Community. Your tests stay where you keep them: in your tests folder, or in your own Firebase project.
