Refund policy

Last updated 28 September 2026.

Breakpatch Team and Business are sold by our reseller Paddle.com, the Merchant of Record for all our orders, which handles refunds. This policy is our promise on top of your rights under the law where you live.

First payment: 14 days, no questions

If Breakpatch isn't right for you, ask for a refund within 14 days of your first payment and you get all of it back.

Renewals aren't refunded

Your subscription renews automatically at the end of each month or year. You can cancel at any time before the renewal date and you won't be charged again. Renewal payments aren't refunded: if you cancel after a renewal, the licence keeps working until the end of that period and then ends.

Changes during a period

Seats and machine licences you add are charged pro rata for the rest of the period; ones you remove are credited pro rata against your next payments. Cancelling stops the next renewal: the licence keeps working until the end of the period you paid for, and isn't refunded.

How to ask

  • Email support@breakpatch.dev from the address you bought with, or
  • find your order at paddle.net and ask Paddle directly.

Refunds go back to the card or account you paid with, usually within 5 to 10 business days. When a whole purchase is refunded, its licence ends and the people on it go back to Breakpatch Community. Your tests stay in your own Firebase project.
