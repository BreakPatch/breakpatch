Install

Thank you. Your licence is on its way.

Paddle, our reseller, emails you the receipt. Your licence key is waiting on your account page: sign in there with a link we email you, no password needed.

  1. Open account.breakpatch.dev and enter the email you bought with. We email you a sign-in link.
  2. Open the link. You see your licence and Your new licence key (shown once). Press Show my licence key and copy it somewhere safe: it isn't shown again.
  3. In Breakpatch, a workspace admin enters the key once, in Settings → Licence.
  4. Invite your team. The invite link sets up each person's licence, so they don't enter a key: they take a seat when they sign in. The local runner and CI machines use a machine licence.

It can take a minute for the licence to appear. If you see “No licences for this email”, wait a moment and reload the page.

On the account page you can also add or remove seats, see invoices and change the payment method (Manage billing). Need help? Email support@breakpatch.dev.
